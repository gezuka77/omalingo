# Public source catalog

Curate short learning sessions for Finnish, English, Hungarian, Traditional Chinese and Japanese.

- Suggest individual lessons, dialogues, short stories, articles, videos and podcast episodes.
- Do not suggest general book catalogs, novels, long collections or complete textbooks. Short graded readers are appropriate.
- Public courses must offer individual lessons or chapter files without a publisher account or payment. Irodori listings exclude the complete book, audio-embedded PDFs and files labelled over 20 MB.
- Browse-only story resources must not import their directory or promotional description as a lesson.
- Check feeds for real items, rather than treating an HTTP 200 response as evidence of a usable feed.
- Interests and followed sources belong to a user and study language; public catalog entries are shared.
- Recommend verified public materials. Retire paid or signup-gated listings and mixed feeds that can route learners to gated items. An explicitly public reading or video section can be listed by its direct link.
- When explicitly requested, newspaper headline feeds may be listed as publisher-only sources with `browseOnly: true` and clear subscription/access labels. Their items open on the publisher’s website and are not offered for direct import. Keep retired importable versions retired.
- Label publisher-only lessons clearly. Preserve the explicit transcript choice before any paid video transcription.
- Trending is personal discovery, not a claim about global popularity.
